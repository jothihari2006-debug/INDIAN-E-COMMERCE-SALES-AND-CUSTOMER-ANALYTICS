## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes Indian e-commerce data from **2023–2025**, covering **100,000 orders, 22,474 customers, and 546 products** across 9 connected tables.

The project provides an end-to-end analysis of **sales, finance, customers, marketing, delivery operations, product quality, inventory, and returns**.

## 📈 Analysis & Key Insights

### 1. Financial & Sales Performance

* **Fashion** is the strongest sales category, followed by Home & Kitchen and Footwear.
* **0% discount** generates the highest average profit.
* Discounts above **15% significantly reduce profitability** and can result in losses.
* **UPI** is the leading payment method by transaction value.
* Delivered orders account for the majority of transaction volume.
* Returns and cancellations are major sources of revenue leakage.

### 2. Customer & Marketing Analytics

* **Gold customers** contribute the highest overall customer value.
* **Active Gold customers** represent one of the strongest individual revenue segments.
* **SMS Marketing** generates the highest average customer spending.
* **Organic Search** is one of the most efficient marketing channels.
* **Maharashtra** has the highest sales concentration, followed by Uttar Pradesh and Gujarat.
* Churned customers represent a significant revenue-retention opportunity.

### 3. Supply Chain & Delivery Operations

* **Standard shipping** has the highest number of delivery delays.
* **Express shipping** is the second-largest source of delays.
* **Patna → Kolkata** is among the slowest fulfillment corridors.
* Delivery times for delivered and returned orders are very similar.
* This indicates that product-related factors may have a greater influence on returns than delivery speed.

### 4. Product Quality & Inventory Management

* **Standard products** contribute the highest sales volume.
* **Fashion** has the highest return volume.
* **Size issue** is the leading reason for product returns.
* Product damage and changed-mind returns are also significant contributors.
* **Pet Supplies** has the highest average customer rating.
* Some brands show noticeable differences between verified and unverified customer ratings.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Maintain a **15% discount limit** to protect profit margins.
* Prioritize high-performing categories such as **Fashion, Home & Kitchen, and Footwear**.
* Increase investment in **Organic Search/SEO**.
* Review inefficient advertising channels such as Facebook.
* Focus regional marketing and distribution on high-value states.
* Improve Standard shipping performance and carrier SLAs.
* Introduce better sizing guides and fit tools to reduce Fashion returns.
* Develop retention strategies for high-value churned customers.
